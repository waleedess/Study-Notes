### Introduction

Reconnaissance is the essential first step in security testing, enabling hackers to map an application's attack surface and avoid wasted effort on irrelevant technologies. Testers should begin with a thorough manual walkthrough—exploring every feature, link, and user privilege level—to understand data entry points and interactions before diving into deeper technical recon. When advancing to automated bash scripts or intrusive techniques like port scanning and spidering, explicit authorization must be verified beforehand to ensure high-traffic scans are permitted.
