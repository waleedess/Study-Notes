
> TOOL: [[https://viewdns.info/]] for both WHOIS & Reverse WHOIS

- WHOIS is a protocol for looking up who registered a domain or IP block. It returns the registrar, creation and expiry dates, name servers, and often the registrant’s name, organization, email, or phone. 
	- Many registrations now show privacy-proxy details instead, because of GDPR redaction and registrar privacy services

- Reverse WHOIS searches the WHOIS database by a field such as registrant name, email address, phone number, or organization. A hit returns every domain that field appears in, which can reveal forgotten or related assets, such as staging sites, old brands, or domains ==registered by the same admin==.
	- Useful as WOHIS information is not always available, as some organizations and individuals use a service called domain privacy, in which a third-party service provider replaces the user’s information with that of a forwarding service.
